Policies made simple!

Five things to know before you lock in. Scroll through.

01

Deposits

A $30 deposit is required to secure an appointment. There is a $2 processing fee that is not applied toward your service.

All monetary items are non-refundable & non-transferable.

02

Late Policy

10-minute grace period. A $1 per minute fee starts at 11 minutes late.

Appointments are subject to cancellation after 10 minutes.

03

After Hours

Appointments starting, ending, or occurring between 8PM – 8AM automatically incur a $25–$50 fee.

For accommodating the convenience of late/overnight services.

04

Rescheduling

You may reschedule 48 hours in advance or more using your initial confirmation email.

05

Cancellation

Cancelling removes your appointment from the books. Your deposit is forfeited and a fee of your remaining balance will be incurred. Leaving your appointment for any reason counts as a cancellation.

No direct written communication = cancellation. On the day of your appointment, reach out when you're on the way and when you arrive.

Things to Remember

Keep scrolling

Remaining Balances

Due at the start of your appointment. Accepted via Tap to Pay, debit/credit card, Zelle & cash only.

Arriving unprepared will result in fees or cancellation.

Extra Guests

Extra guests are welcome if they have a confirmed appointment booked two weeks in advance, directly before or after your appointment time.

Jewels, Not Tools

Your nails are built to last but they're still delicate creations. Not made for picking, fighting, flicking, or opening anything.

Moisturize your hands, oil your cuticles & wear gloves while doing chores!

Masks

You are required to bring a mask! I use low-odor products, however fumes & dust are still present.

Please be considerate of my health & yours, as well as clients looking forward to their appointments with me in good health.
