Why your review count is stuck

Your Happy Customers Aren't Leaving Reviews. Your Unhappy Ones Are.

The problem

Your best work is invisible on Google.

A frustrated customer will go online and post a review the same night. A happy customer means to leave one, then forgets. The job is done, the invoice is paid, and they've moved on. That leaves your profile with fewer reviews than your work deserves, and it lets a few bad days count for more than they should.

Meanwhile, the homeowner searching "roofer near me" compares star ratings and review counts in about ten seconds, then calls the company with more reviews.

  • Most satisfied customers never leave a review unless they're asked at the right moment
  • Asking in person is awkward, and your crew is busy
  • Competitors with more reviews get the call, even when your work is better
The fix

Ask every customer at the right moment, automatically.

As soon as a job is marked complete or an invoice is paid, our system sends your customer a short, personal text and email about their experience. It includes their name, your branding, and a photo, so it looks like it came from you and not from a robot.

One tap takes them straight to your Google review page. Nobody on your team has to remember to ask.

  • Sent automatically after every completed job
  • Personalized with the customer's name and your branding
  • Friendly reminders for customers who don't respond the first time
  • Private feedback option so you hear about problems first and can fix them

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this compliant with Google's review policies?
Yes. Google allows businesses to ask customers for reviews. What it doesn't allow is paying for reviews, writing fake ones, or only asking happy customers while blocking unhappy ones. Our system follows those rules: every customer who completes a job gets the same request and can reach your Google review page, and we never offer incentives for reviews. The private feedback option gives customers an extra way to reach you directly. It doesn't replace their ability to post publicly.
How long does setup take?
Your part takes under 10 minutes. On a short onboarding call, we connect your job or invoicing software, confirm your branding, and approve your message wording. We handle the rest, and most clients are sending their first review requests within 48 hours.
Do my staff need to learn new software?
No. Your team keeps working the way they do now. When they mark a job complete or send an invoice in your current software, the review request goes out on its own. There's an optional mobile app if you want to see your reviews and feedback, but most owners just read the text alerts.
What if a customer has a bad experience?
You'll want to hear about it before anyone else does. When a customer tells us something went wrong, they're offered a short private form that goes straight to you, and you get an instant alert with their name and number. That gives you the chance to call, fix the problem, and often turn an upset customer into a loyal one. The customer can still leave a public Google review if they choose.
How does the custom website build work alongside the review software?
Both run on the same platform, so they're connected from day one. New Google reviews appear on your website automatically. Website chats, quote requests, and booked appointments land in the same inbox as your review alerts. Every customer who books through your site is added to your review follow-up once the job is done. You can start with reviews and add a website later, or bundle both from the start.
