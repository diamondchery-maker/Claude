    From idea to healed

    How a piece happens

    Typical lead time: 3–6 weeks

    1. 1

      Request

      Send your idea, placement and rough size. References help, but they're a starting point, not a copy.

    2. 2

      Consult

      A 20-minute call or visit. We settle size and flow, and you pay a $100 deposit that comes off the final price.

    3. 3

      Drawing

      I draw the piece in the week before your session. You see it on the day and we adjust it together.

    4. 4

      Stencil & session

      The stencil goes on, we check placement standing and moving, and then we tattoo.

    5. 5

      Heal & touch-up

      One free touch-up within 3 months. Send a healed photo so it can go in the book.

    Aftercare

    What healing looks like

    1. Days 1–3Second-skin film stays on. Tender and a little red.
    2. Days 4–14Wash twice a day, thin layer of unscented lotion. It will peel. Don't pick.
    3. Weeks 2–4Looks cloudy while new skin settles. No sun, pools or baths.
    4. Week 6+Fully settled. SPF 50 on it for life keeps fine lines sharp.
